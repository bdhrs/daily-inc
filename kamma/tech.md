## Conventions
- Follow existing patterns in the codebase. Do not introduce new patterns without a clear reason.
- Seed/default data ships as a JSON asset in the store's own format so it can be regenerated by the app's own export, not hand-built in Dart.
- An untouched item (empty history) always targets its `startValue` however old its `startDate` is — the penalty branch in `calculateTodayValue` is undone by the start-anchored clamp. Baked-in start dates are therefore safe for increasing items, but not for decreasing ones (start > end clamps to `endValue`).
- `epochDays`/`dateFromEpochDays` (`graph_style_helpers.dart`) must stay UTC-anchored, not local-midnight, to avoid a timezone-dependent fractional-day offset. `GraphStyle.stepDirection` must stay `1.0` — `0.0` looks correct in isolation but drops the most recent day's bar entirely (no "next" point exists to bound its interval). Any change to either must be verified by rendering on device, not by reading `fl_chart` source alone.
